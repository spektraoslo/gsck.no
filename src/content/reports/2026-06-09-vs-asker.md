**A heartbreaking loss — one run under the D/L method after a rain-shortened
14-over game. So much to be proud of on a night the margins couldn't have
been finer.**

## Fighting Total from a Tricky Start

Asker won the toss and asked us to bat on a wet, tricky evening. Rain
shortened the game to 14 overs a side, and we lost Rijoy on the first ball
of the innings — the toughest possible start.

**Nithin Nandan** stood up when it mattered. His **32 off 22 (five fours, one
six, SR 145)** was the innings that kept us in the contest. Basil Kuriakose
followed with a valuable 14, Ranjit Ramakrishnan Nair chipped in with 11,
and Lineesh Puthiyotttum Kandi struck a quick 13 off 10 down the order to
push us to 108/6.

Given the conditions and the powerplay collapse, that was a competitive
total.

## The Bowlers Nearly Pulled It Off

Defending 109 in a 14-over sprint is brutal — and we came within a single
run of doing it.

**Bibin Thomas was outstanding: 2 overs, 3 wickets for 18** — the spell that
almost broke the chase open. Renju Kochumman Raju took 2/13 from his 3
overs, Bibin Devasia struck twice at the death, and Rohith Moothedath grabbed
a wicket in the middle.

Waqar Ahmad's 39 off 27 for Asker was the difference — a clean-striking
knock that just about carried them home. In the final over, Ahmad Ali Safi's
7 off 2 balls (with a six) sealed it by the finest of margins.

## Positives from the Loss

- Nithin's temperament under pressure — 32 in a rain-shortened chase is a
  captain's knock in everything but name.
- Bibin Thomas showing what he can do with the new ball.
- Multiple wicket-takers again — five bowlers on the sheet.
- Lower-order runs from Lineesh Puthiyotttum Kandi.

One run. One ball. That's how close it was. Take the fight, take the
learnings, and back it up next game.
