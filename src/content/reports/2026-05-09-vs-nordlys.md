**A tough season opener at Bærum. Nordlys got away from us in the middle
overs, and Asim Safi did the rest with the ball. Plenty to build on from
here.**

## Bowling Was Right There Until One Big Partnership

Grorud won the toss and chose to bowl. For most of the innings we were on
top — but **Abhishek Patil's 52 off 37** (four fours, strike rate 140)
carried Nordlys past a par score.

Standout with the ball:

- **Basil Boon Kuriakose: 4 overs, 1 maiden, 14 runs, 1 wicket — economy
  3.50.** Elite T20 bowling. Nothing to score off.
- **Bibin Devasia: 2-0-6-0** (economy 3.00) — even better on the run rate.
- Wickets came from every angle: Renju Kochumman Raju, Ranjith Tom, Rahul
  Kaladharan and Bibin Thomas all struck.

Nordlys ended on **139/7** — a competitive but not out-of-reach total.

## The Chase Never Got Going

Asim Safi (**4/18 from 4 overs**) was the difference. We lost early wickets,
never rebuilt the platform, and slid to 20/5 inside 6 overs.

Fighting knocks from:

- **Rijoy P: 24 off 27** — the top scorer, held one end.
- **Ranjith Tom: 18 off 19** (one four, one six).
- **Lineesh Puthiyotttum Kandi: 14 off 13** — some late resistance.
- **Bibin Devasia: 11 off 10** — kept fighting.

We were bowled out for 102. A day that got away from us.

## Positives to Take From the Opener

- Basil Boon Kuriakose bowling a maiden in a T20, economy 3.50 — bookmark
  that spell.
- Wicket-takers spread across the attack.
- A season is just beginning. First game, first learnings.

Reset and back at it next fixture.
