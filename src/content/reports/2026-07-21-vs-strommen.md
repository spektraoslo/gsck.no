## Kiran and Rijoy Held the Chase Together

We lost two early wickets in the powerplay, but Kiran Raj (**37 off 41**) and
Rijoy P (**36 off 23**, six fours) rebuilt the innings with a partnership
that changed the tempo of the game.

Rijoy went into attack mode when the required rate started to climb. Bibin
Devasia followed up with a lovely cameo of **23 off 17**, and Benny Kuriakose
chipped in with 12 to keep the chase moving.

Grorud got home with 8 balls to spare — 145/6 in 18.4 overs. A calm, composed
chase where the responsibility was passed around like it should be.

## A Real Team Win

Contributions from top, middle and lower order with the bat. Every bowler on
